How It Works

1. Donors

Restaurants, grocery stores, and individuals register on our platform to donate excess food. They specify the food type, quantity, and pickup location through our easy-to-use dashboard.

2. Pickup by Volunteers

Our dedicated volunteers receive notifications about available food in their area. They can review and accept pickup requests directly from the volunteer portal, ensuring timely collection.

3. Distribution

The collected food is securely and hygienically delivered to local shelters, community centers, and directly to people in need, minimizing the time between pickup and drop-off.

4. Making an Impact

Together, we not only reduce greenhouse gas emissions caused by food waste but also provide nutritious meals to thrive. Every contribution counts!
