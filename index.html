Our Mission

Work together with Food Donors, Volunteers, and Recipients to ensure no food goes to waste. Join us in this mission to make a difference and help feed those in need.

  • Connect local donors to volunteers
  • Deliver meals to shelters
  • Reduce global food waste

Flow of Process

1. Donate

Restaurants & individuals register to donate excess food.

2. Collect

Our dedicated volunteers collect the food from donors.

3. Distribute

The collected food is delivered to shelters and people in need.
